01 — Profile

Engineering signals into insight

From the sensor front-end to the trained model — the whole chain, end to end.

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from BRAC University (major: Electronics) and currently a Research Assistant in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering. My work builds machine-learning and computer-vision pipelines over multimodal physiological and environmental sensor data, and deploys them across edge–cloud architectures for real-time inference.

I am comfortable across the full data workflow: sensor instrumentation and calibration, signal processing and feature engineering, model training and validation, and analysis of telemetry from distributed sensor networks. I have authored and co-authored six peer-reviewed Q1 journal publications spanning AI-powered cybersecurity, machine learning and digital-twin simulation for energy systems, and AI/IoT-enabled distributed energy storage.

I am a three-time Dean’s List recipient and I care deeply about engineering education, student mentorship, and contributing to excellence in teaching, research and academic development.
